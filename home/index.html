About Me

My name is Kingsley Shyne Mattis Sogorb, and I am a passionate video game programmer specializing in systems and engine development. I thrive on building complex, modular tools that empower developers to bring their ideas to life efficiently. My goal is to create robust frameworks that enhance productivity and open new creative possibilities.

One of my most notable projects is a modular weapon editor for Unreal Engine, designed to streamline weapon creation through a custom node-based system. By focusing on scalability and usability, I ensure that my tools integrate seamlessly into development pipelines, reducing iteration time and maximizing flexibility.

I am driven by efficiency, problem-solving, and technical challenges. Rather than focusing on moment-to-moment gameplay logic, I prefer architecting systems that others can rely on—whether it's procedural generation, networking, or engine optimizations. My approach is always about delivering clean, well-structured, and highly performant solutions.

When I'm not programming, I enjoy diving into game design theory, refining my swordsmanship skills, training dogs, and writing stories. Each of these passions influences how I approach development—whether it's understanding combat mechanics, designing AI behaviors, or crafting immersive worlds.
