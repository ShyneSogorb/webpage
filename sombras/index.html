Sombras

Sombras is a dark fantasy novel that I am currently writing. Where mutants called shadows have goals that will complete no matter what. The story is set in a world where good intentions crumble, where the characters are faced with moral dilemmas and difficult choices.

"Sombras" invites you into a world of mystery, magic, and untold secrets. Step into an intricate fantasy realm where diverse races, arcane forces, and hidden truths shape the fate of those who dare to seek them.

Follow Yhtia and Avdey as they navigate a perilous journey, uncovering dark conspiracies and confronting forces beyond their understanding. With rich, immersive storytelling and vivid world-building, this tale will transport you to distant lands, making you feel every triumph and tragedy along the way.

Loyalty, sacrifice, and redemption intertwine in a narrative that will keep you captivated until the very last page. Are you ready to step into the shadows and uncover what lies within?"

The novel is still in its early stages, but I am excited about the direction it is taking. I hope to share more about it as I continue to write.
